# Story Canon

The RPG is fully playable without AI. This directory contains the authoritative handcrafted canon used by Classic Mode.

## Core premise

Three centuries ago, the world survived an event called the **First Silence**. For thirteen minutes, every living thing lost its shadow, every bell rang backward, and the sky opened above the old capital. The rulers of the age claimed the event was a natural catastrophe.

It was not.

Beneath the capital is an ancient machine called the **Hollow Star**, built to contain something that was never meant to exist in the human world. The machine has begun to fail. Someone is sabotaging the seals, someone else is trying to repair them, and a third faction wants the Hollow Star opened.

The player begins as an unknown traveler arriving at the gates of the Ashen Capital on the same night three people disappear from different parts of the city.

## Canon rules

1. The game never requires AI to follow the main story.
2. Major plot facts, character identities, world rules, quest outcomes, and endings are deterministic.
3. AI may later provide optional wording or improvised conversation, but it may not contradict this canon.
4. Choices can permanently alter locations, relationships, factions, quest availability, and endings.
5. There is no single morally correct route. Several endings are intentionally bittersweet or ambiguous.

## Story shape

- Prologue — The Bell That Rang Backward
- Act I — Ashes at the Gate
- Act II — The City Beneath the City
- Act III — Roads of the Broken Crown
- Act IV — The Hollow Star
- Act V — The Last Choice
- Epilogues — outcome-dependent

The currently implemented handcrafted Chapter 1 targets roughly 45–75 minutes for a first playthrough, depending on investigation choices and optional encounters. The full campaign is designed around roughly three hours for a focused first playthrough, with substantially more time available for exploration, optional quests, secrets, and alternate routes.
